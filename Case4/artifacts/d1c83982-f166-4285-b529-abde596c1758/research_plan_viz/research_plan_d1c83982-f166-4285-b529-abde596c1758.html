📋 Impact of Hazardous Waste Proximity on Asthma Prevalence in North Carolina

Research Question: How does proximity to hazardous waste facilities influence spatial patterns of asthma prevalence across census tracts of North Carolina, and how do these relationships vary spatially by socioeconomic and demographic context?

To quantify and interpret how proximity to hazardous waste facilities affects asthma prevalence while accounting for demographic and socioeconomic variability across North Carolina census tracts

🎯 Objective 1: Understand the spatial relationship between hazardous waste facility proximity and asthma prevalence in North Carolina census tracts (3 steps)
▼
Hypothesis: Census tracts with closer proximity to hazardous waste facilities exhibit higher asthma prevalence rates, controlling for basic demographic factors
1 Generate centroids for census tract geometries
2 Calculate distance from tract centroids to nearest hazardous waste facility
3 Join tract hazard distances with asthma prevalence data using GEOID
🎯 Objective 2: Quantify how socioeconomic and demographic factors modify the relationship between environmental proximity and asthma outcomes (3 steps)
▼
Hypothesis: The association between hazardous waste proximity and asthma prevalence varies significantly by median household income and racial composition across census tracts
1 Select socioeconomic variables: median_household_income, percent_minority, percent_children
2 Integrate demographic variables with existing spatial and health data using GEOID
3 Analyze spatially varying relationships between proximity (hazard_meters), demographics (minority_pct) and asthma (prevalence_rate)
🎯 Objective 3: Identify census tracts with statistically significant high-risk clusters of environmental exposure and health outcomes (2 steps)
▼
Hypothesis: Tracts with < 20% minority populations will show stronger spatial clustering of high-risk combinations
1 Calculate Gi* statistics for combined variables hazard_meters and asthma_prevalence_rate
2 Compare median minority_pct between hotspot and non-hotspot tracts using Kruskal-Wallis H-test
